# What's new in 0.8

<Callout type="info">
  <Premium /> marks features that need a plan on the hosted service. A self-hosted server includes them (smart search also needs an AI Gateway key), and each person turns them on under Settings › Experimental.
</Callout>

## A résumé and cover letter for each application <Premium />

Open an application and look at its **Documents** card. It now starts with one row each for the résumé and the cover letter in use, and says where each came from: your main one, a named version, or tailored for this application.

- **Change** lists your versions and puts the one you pick on this application. Your main one is always free to use.
- **Compare** opens a side-by-side diff against your main one, in one column on a phone. It compares the text, so layout-only changes don't show.

<Clip src="/docs/whats-new/0-8/compare" width={720} height={462} caption="Choosing a version for an application, then comparing it with your main résumé." />

Read more in the [API reference](/api-reference): `POST /v1/applications/{id}/documents` and `GET /v1/applications/{id}/compare`.

## Smart search and suggested answers <Premium />

Search by meaning, not just by words. Your saved answers, your interview-round prep and notes, and your text documents are all searched.

- **Profile › Questions:** while you search, a **Related by meaning** section lists matches in other words.
- **Add question:** as you type, **Close to things you've already written** offers your earlier answers, with **Use this**.
- **Prepare:** for a form question with no exact match, Pursuit keeps the closest saved answer as a suggestion. The question still waits for you in **needs input**; nothing is filled in silently.

<Clip src="/docs/whats-new/0-8/search" width={720} height={544} caption="Searching for “conflict” finds the answer about a disagreement." />
